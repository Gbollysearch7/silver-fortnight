## FUNDING MODEL
Our challenges are based on **demo accounts with virtual capital** throughout the whole challenge journey. After you pass Funded Level, you sign a **Signal Provider Agreement** — TradersYard then copies your trades as signals to its corporate account (if internal risk assessments are met). At no point are you held accountable for losses, and at no point do you trade with real money. All challenge activity is virtual.
- Entity: **TradersYard GmbH, Kärntnerring 5-7, 1010 Vienna, Austria**. VAT-ID: ATU77254506.

## WHO CAN JOIN / COUNTRY RESTRICTIONS
- Accepts traders worldwide, 18+, no special qualification. Does NOT serve sanctioned countries/persons, financial-crime records, or previously banned users.
- Futures challenges: must confirm you are a **non-professional** trader.
- FX restricted countries (Rise): see riseworks.io supported-countries list (policy update Dec 2025). Crypto payout via Veriff KYC may bypass some bans.

## DEMO / PRACTICE
- **No pre-challenge paper trading.** "you cannot have a paper trading account before you enter a challenge." Free **Tournaments** give a practice-like active account instead.

## PROFIT SPLIT (scalable)
- First $300 → 100% | $300–$1,000 → 90% | above $1,000 → 80%.
- Example: $1,200 profit → $300 (100%) + $630 (90% of next $700) + $160 (80% of $200) = **$1,090**.

## PAYOUTS
- Min payout **$50** (instant funded: $50 or 2% of balance, whichever greater). **14-day cycle** (first after 15 days).
- Processed 1–2 business days after KYC; **most within 4–6 business hours** of request.
- KYC: Rise (bank or crypto, no invoice) OR Veriff (crypto only, invoice required).
- Crypto options: BTC, ETH, LTC, USDC (Ethereum), USDT (Tron). FIAT via Rise.
- No payout cap on FX. Futures first 5 payouts capped: 25K→$2k, 50K→$3k, 100K→$4k, 125K→$5k. Futures cycle max 10 payouts or $40k, then 30-day cooldown.
- Futures payout cadence: first after 10 days, then every 10 days. Instant Funded: first after 15 days, then every 10; daily withdrawals after 60 days good standing.

## PROHIBITED PRACTICES (banned)
- **Copy trading** | Hedging across accounts | Arbitrage/latency exploitation | Martingale/grid | Gambling behavior | News-trading abuse | VPN/VPS.
- Violations → profit deduction or account termination.
- Only **one** challenge account can be connected at a time.

## RULES
- **40% Consistency Rule:** Best Day Profit ≤ 40% of Total Closed Profit (closed trades only). Doesn't fail you, but blocks pass/payout until ratio drops below 40%.
- **No time limits** on challenges or funded accounts.
- **Inactivity:** must trade once per 30 days or account permanently closed.
- **News:** restricted 10 min before / 5 min after high-impact; always restricted on funded.
- **Martingale rule:** new position ≤ 2× largest losing trade in a streak.
- **Max margin per trade:** ≤ 70% of account balance (breach forfeits that trade's profit).
- **Drawdown types:** Daily (equity, resets UTC 00:00) | Static (fixed, never moves) | EoD Max (trails up only, locks at max). Daily & Max are equity-based (include open P/L).
- **Leverage:** user-selected at purchase. FX up to 1:75; commodities/indices 1:15; stocks/crypto 1:2.

## FEES / GUARANTEE / ACCOUNTS
- One entry fee, no hidden fees (datafeed/platform/infra covered). Payment: Visa/MC/Amex/Maestro/Diners/Crypto via Stripe.
- **14-day money-back guarantee** — full refund of entry fee IF no orders/trades placed yet.
- Failed account → 10% discount coupon for a NEW purchase (not a reset; no progress carried over).
- Unlimited challenge accounts; max funded allocation **$300k or 2 funded accounts** (whichever first). Malaysia/Pakistan/Indonesia capped at **$100k**.
- Scaling (FX 1/2-step): +10% base size every 4th payout of ≥1% profit, max $200k or 5× initial. Futures: no scaling.

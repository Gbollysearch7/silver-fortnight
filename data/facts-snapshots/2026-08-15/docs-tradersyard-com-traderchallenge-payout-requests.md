# Payout Requests

Once your account has reached **Funded Level**, you become eligible to request payouts. The **minimum payout amount is 50 USD.**

Specifically for instant funded accounts, the minimum withdrawable payout is $50 or 2% of the initial balance, whichever is **greater**

Our **payout cycle is 14 days**, and the countdown toward your next available payout can be viewed directly on your dashboard.

<figure><img src="/files/K7tLlIMh5celSf7vJUbO" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The section labeled “Potential Payout” reflects the exact amount you will be able to withdraw once you submit a payout request.

### **How to Request a Payout**

When your 14-day countdown has ended, a **Payout button** will automatically appear on your dashboard.\
Clicking this button will direct you to the payout request form.

<figure><img src="/files/BICZahLD29uqclmT4XsS" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

🔗 **Payout Request Form:** [**LINK**](https://payout-form.tradersyard.com/)<br>

Afterwards the request-payout button will be disabled on your dashboard again until you qualify for your next payout.

Inside the form, you will be asked to choose how you want to complete your KYC (Know Your Customer). You may select **one** of the following:

<table><thead><tr><th>Rise KYC</th><th>Veriff KYC</th><th data-hidden></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>No invoice needed</td><td>Invoice <strong>required</strong></td><td></td></tr><tr><td><p>Funds can be withdrawn directly to:</p><ul><li>Your <strong>bank account</strong>, or</li><li>Your <strong>crypto wallet</strong></li></ul></td><td>Payment is sent <strong>directly to your crypto wallet.</strong></td><td></td></tr></tbody></table>

After submitting the form, you will receive a **separate email invitation** to complete the KYC process using the provider you selected.

{% hint style="warning" %}
You need to wait until you got your payout-confirmation until you are allowed to start trading in your next level. If you start trading on the next level before that, your challenge account might be terminated and is not eligible for any further payouts.
{% endhint %}

## FAQ

<details>

<summary>❓ How fast are the payouts?</summary>

We process payouts in 1-2 business days after KYC approval. During public holidays, no payouts are processed. Most traders receive their profit-share payments within 4-6 business-hours of requesting a payout.

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ Do I need to send an invoice?</summary>

If you are using Rise, then Rise will automatically generate the invoice. However, if you are using Veriff, then you need to **create an invoice** issued by you to TradersYard for the signal provider services rendered:

* The invoice must comply with the legal requirements of your country.
* The invoice must include the following details:
  * **Invoice recipient:** TradersYard GmbH, Kärntnerring 5-7, 1010 Vienna, Austria, VAT-ID: ATU77254506
  * **Invoice sender:** The name of the person who paid the challenge registration fee.
  * Invoice numer: For example, 01 for your first payout, 02 for your second payout etc.
  * Your crypto wallet address. Please note we offer the following cyrpto payout options: BTC, ETH, LTC, USDC (Ethereum), USDT (Tron)
  * **Invoice date:** Date the invoice is issued.
  * **Service period:** The period from when you purchased the challenge to when you completed the current level.
  * **Service description:** Performance commission for account number – (your TradersYard account number, format: TC-X-XXXXXX)
  * **Amount:** Specify the exact payout amount you wish to request.
  * **Tax rate:** Indicate the applicable tax rate, even if it is 0%. For invoices from the EU, add a reverse charge note if applicable.

You can use tools such as <https://invoiceninja.com/>, <https://runbill.com/>, or <https://sevdesk.at/> to create your invoice.

Make sure it is in PDF-format and upload it onto the payouts form.

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ What happens if I fail my KYC?</summary>

We do not have control over the KYC verification outcome. Completing KYC successfully is required before we can issue any payments.

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ Do I also get a scale-up when requesting a payout?</summary>

You can find all rules when you can request a scale-up of your account-size here: <https://docs.tradersyard.com/traderchallenge/account-scaling>

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ Do I need to reach 10% profit to request a payout?</summary>

No. You may request a payout as long as you meet the **minimum requirement of 50 USD**.\
However, if you prefer to withdraw more than 10% profits, you're free to do so—**the sky is the limit**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ I submitted my KYC but it still says “Pending.” What should I do?</summary>

Your KYC is being reviewed by the provider you selected (Rise or Veriff). We are **not able to speed up or influence the verification process**, as it is handled entirely by the KYC provider.\
Please wait until they complete their checks and approve your verification. Once approved, your payout will be processed.

</details>

<details>

<summary>❓ How much percent of the profit I made am I receiving?</summary>

TradersYard uses a scalable profit split system, where your payout percentage depends on the amount of profit generated.

* First **$300** → **100%**
* Next **$700** ($300–$1,000) → **90%**
* Any profit above **$1,000** → **80%**

**Example:**\
If you request a payout with **$1,200 profit**:

* First $300 at 100% = $300
* Next $700 at 90% = $630
* Remaining $200 at 80% = $160

**Total payout = $1,090 out of $1,200 profit.**

</details>

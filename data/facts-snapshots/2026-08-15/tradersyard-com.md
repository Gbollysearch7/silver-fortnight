## Frequently Asked Questions

What makes TradersYard different from other prop firms?

We focus on transparent rules, reliable technology and responsive support. Every challenge is clearly explained upfront, so you always know what is expected.

How does TradersYard work?

Choose a challenge, trade on a simulated account and meet the required objectives without breaching the rules. Once you pass, you can receive a funded account and earn rewards based on your performance.

What assets can I trade?

You can trade forex, commodities, indices, stocks, ETFs, cryptocurrencies and futures. Available instruments may vary by challenge type. [View the full list of tradable instruments](https://tradersyard.com/symbols).

What are the rules?

Rules vary by challenge which includes a profit target, daily drawdown, maximum drawdown and minimum profitable days requirements. All applicable rules are shown before purchase and also inside your dashboard. No hidden rules. You must also follow our Prohibited Practices policy to avoid rule violations or misuse. [View the detailed explanation on rules.](https://tradersyard.com/rules)

How fast are the rewards?

We process rewards in 1–2 business days after KYC approval. During public holidays no rewards are processed. Most traders receive their profit-share payments within 4–6 business hours of requesting a reward.

What is the profit split?

For FX and Futures funded accounts, you keep 100% of the first $300, 90% from $300 to $1,000 and 80% of profits above $1,000.

Is my country eligible?

Traders from most countries can participate. Availability is subject to local regulations and restricted jurisdictions; eligibility is confirmed during registration or checkout.

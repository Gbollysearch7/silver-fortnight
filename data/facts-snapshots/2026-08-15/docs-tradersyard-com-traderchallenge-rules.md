# Rules

You can find all rules for TradersYard challenges here: <https://tradersyard.com/rules>

**Note:** If any violation is identified under the Trading Rules, a formal warning may be issued and/or the account's profit target may be adjusted accordingly. If you believe a profit target adjustment was applied incorrectly or the violation determination is unclear, please create a support ticket for review and appeal.

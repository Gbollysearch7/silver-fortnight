# What Prop Firms Use DXtrade? How to Check in 2026

A large share of forex and CFD prop firms now run DXtrade, the white-label trading platform built by Devexperts. So the honest answer to **what prop firms use DXtrade** is: a lot of them, the list changes constantly, and any published name list goes stale within months.

So instead of a list that expires, this guide covers what DXtrade actually is, why so many prop firms adopted it, what trading on it feels like, and how to confirm a firm's platform before you pay an evaluation fee.

## DXtrade Is a White-Label Platform, Not a Prop Firm

First, clear up the most common confusion. DXtrade is not a broker and not a prop firm. It is a SaaS trading platform developed by [Devexperts](https://devexperts.com), a financial software company that builds trading technology for brokers and institutions.

Prop firms and brokers license DXtrade, put their own logo and colours on it, and present it as their own trading environment. This is a classic [white-label](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/w/white-label-product.asp) arrangement: the technology comes from one company, the brand you see belongs to another.

That is why two different prop firms can both "have their own platform" that looks suspiciously similar. Underneath, it is often the same DXtrade engine with different paint.

If you are still getting your head around the funded trading model itself, read our guide on [what a prop firm is](https://tradersyard.com/blog-posts/what-is-a-prop-firm) first. The platform question makes far more sense once you understand that prop firm evaluations run in simulated environments, not on live client accounts.

## Why Prop Firms With DXtrade Became So Common

DXtrade existed well before the prop industry noticed it. The turning point came in early 2024, when many prop firms lost or gave up access to MT4 and MT5 white labels and needed a replacement platform quickly.

DXtrade fit that moment for three reasons.

**It deploys fast.** As a hosted SaaS product, a firm can license DXtrade and get a branded platform in front of traders far quicker than building technology from scratch.

**The firm controls the environment.** Prop firms run evaluations on simulated accounts, so they need direct control over instruments, leverage, spreads, and risk settings. DXtrade was built to give the operator that control.

**It suits the sim and funded account model.** Prop evaluations are not live brokerage accounts. A platform designed to be operated by the firm, rather than tied to one broker's live infrastructure, maps neatly onto how challenges and funded accounts actually work.

The result: prop firms with DXtrade went from rare to routine in roughly a year. Today it sits alongside a handful of other platforms as one of the default choices for forex and CFD prop firms.

## What Trading on DXtrade Actually Feels Like

If you have only ever traded on MetaTrader, expect an adjustment period.

**It is web-first.** You trade in the browser, with no mandatory desktop install. Mobile apps exist for iOS and Android, and the interface generally feels more modern than the older MetaTrader terminals.

**Charting is built in.** DXtrade ships with its own charting, including standard indicators, drawing tools, and multiple chart layouts. For discretionary traders working from clean price charts, it covers the essentials.

**Order handling is straightforward.** Market, limit, and stop orders, position management, and account metrics are all where you would expect them.

The sensible move before paying any fee is to test the environment first. ## The Trade-Offs Traders Talk About Most

No platform is neutral ground, and DXtrade has well-known friction points that come up constantly in trader communities. These are commonly discussed trade-offs, not measured statistics, so weigh them against your own style.

**Automation is the big one.** MetaTrader built a massive ecosystem of Expert Advisors, custom indicators, and copy tools over two decades. DXtrade has nothing comparable. If your edge lives in an MQL-based EA, it will not simply transfer, and automation options on DXtrade are far more limited.

**Custom tooling is thinner.** Traders who rely on niche community-built indicators often find the DXtrade library does not have an equivalent.

**The feel is different.** Some traders adapt in a day. Others never warm to it and prefer the MetaTrader workflow they have used for years. You want to discover your reaction before your evaluation starts, not during it.

Here is how the platform situation typically breaks down for prop traders in 2026:

| Proprietary platform | Built and run by the firm itself | Depends entirely on the firm | Used by firms that want full ownership, like TradersYard |

## How to Confirm a Firm Runs DXtrade Before You Pay

Because platform line-ups change quickly, verify directly instead of trusting a listicle. Five minutes of checking beats a wasted entry fee.

- **Read the firm's platform or FAQ page.** Most firms state their platform openly. If a firm hides basic platform information, treat that as a warning sign in itself.
- **Look at the trading portal login.** DXtrade white labels often reveal themselves through the login page layout, a "powered by DXtrade" note, or a dx.trade address in the URL.
- **Ask support the direct question.** "Is your platform DXtrade, MetaTrader, or your own build?" A trustworthy firm answers in one line.
- **Check for a free way to test.** Tournaments, trials, or demo access let you confirm the platform and its feel before money moves.
- **Search recent trader discussions.** Communities usually know within days when a firm switches platforms. Just check the dates, because posts older than a year are often wrong by now.

Platform transparency is also a decent proxy for overall firm quality. The same due diligence mindset applies when asking whether [are prop firms legit](https://tradersyard.com/blog-posts/are-prop-firms-legit) in general: firms that are open about their technology, rules, and payouts tend to be open about everything else.

## FAQ

### Is DXtrade a broker or a prop firm?

Neither. DXtrade is a trading platform built by Devexperts and licensed to brokers and prop firms as a white-label product. The firm you sign up with operates the account; DXtrade only supplies the technology behind it.

### Can I run EAs or trading bots on DXtrade?

Not the way you can on MetaTrader. DXtrade has no MQL ecosystem, so Expert Advisors built for MT4 or MT5 will not run on it. Its limited automation options are the most common criticism traders raise.

### Why did prop firms move to DXtrade instead of staying on MetaTrader?

After the industry's shift away from MetaTrader in early 2024, firms needed platforms they could deploy fast and control fully. DXtrade offered a hosted, brandable platform suited to simulated evaluation accounts, so many firms adopted it.

### How do I know if a prop firm uses DXtrade before signing up?

Check the firm's platform page, inspect the trading portal login for DXtrade branding or a dx.trade URL, and ask support directly. Recent trader community threads help too, as long as they are current.

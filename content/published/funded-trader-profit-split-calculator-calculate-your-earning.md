# Funded Trader Profit Split Calculator: Calculate Your Earnings

If you’re looking to understand your earnings as a funded trader, the funded trader profit split calculator is an essential tool. This calculator allows you to assess how much of your profits you will retain after accounting for the profit split terms set by your prop trading firm, such as TradersYard. Understanding this concept not only helps you keep your finances in check but also empowers you to strategize effectively.

## Understanding Profit Splits in Prop Trading

In prop trading, particularly with firms like TradersYard, the profit split is crucial to your earnings. The general structure at TradersYard includes an attractive 80% profit split. This means for every dollar you earn in profit, you retain 80 cents, while the firm garners the remaining 20 cents. This model incentivizes traders to perform, as the more they profit, the higher their share. 

Calculating your expected earnings involves recognizing how profit splits operate. You begin with the total profit you’ve generated and then apply the split percentage. For instance, if your trading account showed a profit of $10,000, your take-home would be $8,000 after the profit split. It's important to incorporate this into your trading plans, especially for long-term sustainability and financial planning.

## How to Use the Funded Trader Profit Split Calculator

Using the funded trader profit split calculator is straightforward. First, you’ll need to input your total profit from your trading activities. Once you enter this figure, the calculator will automatically compute your earnings after the profit split based on your firm's specifications. 

Most calculators will also provide additional information like breaking down profits according to different trading challenges—whether you’re using a 1-Step or 2-Step challenge. Understanding the nuances in these challenges can greatly affect how profits accumulate over time. For example, the 1-Step challenge may encourage rapid gains, while the 2-Step challenge is often perceived as more conservative with a slower profit accrual.

For traders who are keen on maximizing their earnings, regularly checking your profit levels using the calculator can provide insight into your trading strategies. This proactive approach allows you to adjust your tactics promptly to align with your financial goals.

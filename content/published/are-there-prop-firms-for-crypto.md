# Are There Prop Firms For Crypto? Your Complete Guide to Crypto Funded Trading

If you're wondering **are there prop firms for crypto**, the answer is a resounding yes. The cryptocurrency market has exploded in recent years, and with it, a new breed of proprietary trading firms has emerged to fund skilled crypto traders. These firms offer traders the opportunity to trade digital assets with substantial capital without risking their own money.

But here's what most traders don't realize: crypto prop trading operates quite differently from traditional forex or stock prop trading. The opportunities are unique, the challenges are specific, and knowing how to navigate this landscape can mean the difference between securing a funded account and wasting time on unsuitable platforms.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through everything you need to know about crypto prop firms, how to find legitimate ones, and the exact steps to get funded.

## What Are Crypto Proprietary Trading Firms?

**Crypto prop firms** are companies that provide capital to traders specifically for trading cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other digital assets. Unlike traditional prop firms that focus on forex, stocks, or futures, these firms specialize in the 24/7 crypto markets.

Here's how they work:

- You complete an evaluation or challenge (usually by meeting profit targets while following risk rules)
- Once you pass, the firm funds your trading account with their capital
- You trade cryptocurrencies and keep a percentage of the profits (typically 60-90%)
- The firm takes on the financial risk while you provide the trading skill

The [cryptocurrency derivatives market](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/cryptocurrency.asp) has grown to handle billions in daily volume, making it attractive for prop firms to deploy capital through skilled traders.

## Why Crypto Prop Firms Are Different From Traditional Prop Firms

Before diving into how to join crypto prop firms, you need to understand what makes them unique:

**Market Hours:** Crypto markets never close. This means more opportunities but also requires different risk management strategies than traditional markets.

**Volatility:** Cryptocurrency price swings can be significantly larger than forex or stocks. A 5-10% daily move isn't uncommon for major cryptocurrencies.


![Why Crypto Prop Firms Are Different From Traditional Prop Firms - Are There Prop Firms For Crypto](https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0a8f1242/3aH2WQbPwZspRsVldIsmH_image.png)


**Asset Variety:** You're not limited to major pairs. Many crypto prop firms allow trading of 50+ cryptocurrency pairs, DeFi tokens, and derivatives.

**Platform Options:** While traditional prop firms often use MetaTrader, crypto firms typically offer specialized platforms or even allow popular crypto exchanges.

> **Important Note:** Due to regulatory considerations, many traditional prop firms (including TradersYard) focus primarily on forex, indices, and commodities rather than cryptocurrencies. Always verify what assets a firm offers before beginning any evaluation.

## Step-by-Step: How to Find Legitimate Crypto Prop Firms

Finding legitimate crypto prop firms requires due diligence. The crypto space has its share of scams, so follow these steps carefully.

### Step 1: Understand the Prerequisites

Before searching for crypto prop firms, ensure you meet these basic requirements:


![Step-by-Step: How to Find Legitimate Crypto Prop Firms - Are There Prop Firms For Crypto](https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0a8f1244/EavonAyPtI4pgQt0Gp1kc_image.png)


- **Trading Experience:** Most firms require at least 6-12 months of crypto trading experience
- **Technical Setup:** Stable internet connection, suitable computer, and familiarity with crypto trading platforms
- **Capital for Evaluation:** You'll need $100-$500 to purchase a challenge or evaluation program
- **Risk Management Knowledge:** Understanding of position sizing, stop losses, and drawdown limits
- **Market Knowledge:** Familiarity with [cryptocurrency fundamentals and technical analysis](https://www.tradingview.com/markets/cryptocurrencies/)

### Step 2: Research Available Crypto Prop Firms

Start by creating a list of potential firms. Here's how to research effectively:

**Create a Comparison Spreadsheet** with these columns:

- Firm name
- Evaluation cost
- Funding amounts available
- Profit split percentage
- Maximum drawdown allowed
- Daily drawdown limits
- Tradeable cryptocurrencies
- Platform requirements
- Payout frequency
- Verification status

**Check Multiple Review Sources:**

1. TrustPilot and similar review platforms
2. Reddit communities (r/CryptoCurrency, r/Daytrading)
3. Trading forums and Discord servers
4. YouTube reviews from verified traders

**Red Flags to Watch For:**

- No verifiable payout proofs
- Extremely difficult evaluation rules
- Unclear terms and conditions
- No responsive customer support
- Recent establishment with no track record
- Suspiciously low evaluation costs

### Step 3: Verify Regulatory Status and Legitimacy

This is critical. Many crypto firms operate in regulatory grey areas.

**Check These Elements:**

1. **Company Registration:** Look up the firm's business registration in their claimed jurisdiction
2. **Physical Address:** Legitimate firms have verifiable business addresses
3. **Team Transparency:** Real companies have identifiable team members on LinkedIn
4. **Payout Proof:** Look for verified payout screenshots with transaction IDs you can check on blockchain explorers

**Key Questions to Ask:**

- How many active funded traders do you currently have?
- Can you provide verifiable blockchain transaction proofs of payouts?
- What platform do funded traders use?
- How are funds secured (cold storage, insurance, etc.)?
- What happens if the platform experiences a technical issue during my trades?

### Step 4: Analyze the Evaluation Requirements

Once you've shortlisted 3-5 firms, deeply analyze their evaluation requirements.

**Compare These Factors:**

| Factor | What to Look For | Why It Matters |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Profit Target | 8-15% is reasonable | Too high suggests few traders pass |
| Max Drawdown | 10-15% is standard | Too tight makes passing extremely difficult |
| Daily Drawdown | 5% minimum | Crypto volatility requires breathing room |
| Trading Days Required | 5-10 days | Ensures you're not lucky, but skilled |
| Holding Over Weekend | Should be allowed | Crypto is 24/7, restrictions are artificial |
| Maximum Position Size | 5-10% per trade | Prevents excessive risk |

**Calculate Your True Cost:**

Don't just look at the evaluation fee. Calculate:

- Evaluation cost × Expected attempts to pass (be realistic - many traders need 2-3 attempts)
- Monthly subscription fees (some firms charge these)
- Profit split percentage impact on your earnings
- Withdrawal fees and minimum payout thresholds

### Step 5: Test With the Smallest Account First

Never start with the largest evaluation. Here's the smart approach:

**Start Small:**

1. Purchase the smallest evaluation available (usually $10,000-$25,000 account size)
2. This minimizes your financial risk while testing the firm
3. You'll learn the platform, rules, and firm responsiveness

**During Your First Evaluation:**

- Test customer support with questions
- Note platform stability and execution speed
- Verify rules are clearly explained and enforced consistently
- Check if the firm provides adequate trading data and analytics

**Pass the Evaluation, Then Verify:**

- Complete the verification process
- Make a small profit on your funded account
- Request your first payout (even if small)
- Verify the payout process works smoothly

Only after successfully receiving your first payout should you consider scaling up to larger account sizes with that firm.

## Common Crypto Prop Firm Models

Understanding the business models helps you choose the right fit.

### Challenge-Based Model

This is the most common model for crypto prop firms:


![Common Crypto Prop Firm Models - Are There Prop Firms For Crypto](https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0a8f1245/6Zy1D6Ec2rViMSgYae4Ya_image.png)


- Pay upfront for a challenge/evaluation
- Meet profit targets within risk parameters
- Pass evaluation and receive funded account
- Keep 60-90% of profits

**Best For:** Traders who are confident in their strategy and can handle evaluation pressure.

### Instant Funding Model

A newer model gaining popularity:

- Higher upfront cost
- Receive funded account immediately
- Must follow strict rules or lose access
- Usually smaller profit splits initially

**Best For:** Experienced traders who want to skip evaluations.

### Profit-Share Only Model

Rare in crypto, more common in traditional markets:

- No upfront cost
- Extensive vetting process
- Lower profit splits (30-50%)
- More like employment than independent trading

**Best For:** Traders with strong track records but limited capital.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues With Crypto Prop Firms

Even with legitimate firms, issues can arise. Here's how to handle them.

### Issue #1: Evaluation Failed Due to Technical Error

**Problem:** Platform glitches, connectivity issues, or incorrect rule violations flagged.

**Solution Steps:**

1. Screenshot everything immediately (error messages, account status, trade history)
2. Record exact times and dates
3. Contact support via email (creates paper trail) and urgent channels
4. Reference specific rule sections from their terms
5. Request review by senior management
6. If unresolved, request refund or free retry

**Prevention:** Always trade with stable internet, keep backup connection, and save daily account screenshots.

### Issue #2: Payout Delays or Issues

**Problem:** Approved payouts not received or indefinitely "processing."

**Solution Steps:**

1. Check payment method provided is correct
2. Verify identity documents are approved
3. Confirm minimum payout threshold met
4. Review payout schedule in terms (some pay bi-weekly, monthly)
5. After 48 hours past stated timeline, escalate to management
6. Document all communication
7. As last resort, post factual account of delays in public reviews (often accelerates resolution)

**Prevention:** Request small first payout to test system before accumulating large balance.

### Issue #3: Unclear Rule Violations

**Problem:** Account closed or evaluation failed for vague rule violations.

**Solution Steps:**

1. Request specific trades and timestamps that violated rules
2. Review exact rule language in original terms
3. Cross-reference with [regulatory guidelines for financial trading](https://www.cftc.gov/digitalassets)
4. If legitimately unclear, request arbitration or appeal process
5. Share factual experience in trader communities to warn others

**Prevention:** Before each trade, review key rules. When in doubt, ask support before trading rather than apologizing after.

### Issue #4: Platform Execution Problems

**Problem:** Slippage, requotes, or platform freezing during high volatility.

**Solution Steps:**

1. Document with screenshots and trade ticket numbers
2. Compare execution with independent price sources (TradingView, CoinMarketCap)
3. Calculate financial impact of poor execution
4. Request compensation or adjustment to account
5. If pattern continues, consider it a red flag about firm legitimacy

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Do crypto prop firms require KYC verification?**

Yes, legitimate crypto prop firms require identity verification to comply with financial regulations. This typically includes government ID and proof of address. Firms that don't require KYC may be operating illegally or could be scams.

**Can I trade NFTs or DeFi tokens with crypto prop firms?**

Most crypto prop firms focus on established cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, and major altcoins. NFT and obscure DeFi token trading is usually restricted due to liquidity concerns and risk management requirements.

**What's the average pass rate for crypto prop firm evaluations?**

Industry estimates suggest 5-15% of participants pass evaluations on their first attempt. Crypto evaluations tend to have lower pass rates than forex due to higher volatility making drawdown management more challenging.

**Can I use trading bots with crypto prop firms?**

This varies by firm. Many prohibit fully automated trading or high-frequency strategies. Always check specific terms, as bot trading that violates rules will result in account termination without refund.

**How long does it take to receive payouts from crypto prop firms?**

Legitimate firms typically process payouts within 1-14 days, with most averaging 3-7 business days. Cryptocurrency payouts are often faster than traditional banking transfers. Delays beyond 14 days without explanation are red flags.
